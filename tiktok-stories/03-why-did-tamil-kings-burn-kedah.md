# Why did Tamil kings burn Kedah in 1025?

> **HOOK (first 3s):** In 1025, Tamil ships sailed 3,000 km to steal a jewelled gate. The excuse was trade.

**Length:** 80s • **Region:** Kedah Srivijaya • **Century:** 11th • **For:** TikTok / Reels / Shorts

---

Rajendra Chola didn't want land. He wanted the strait.

Srivijaya — Palembang on Sumatra — controlled the Malacca Strait the way Singapore does now. If you wanted Chinese silk from Song Canton, you paid Palembang.

The Cholas of Tamil Nadu had their own ships in Nagapattinam and a Song court embassy that Srivijaya kept blocking. So in 1025, Rajendra did what Cholas did best: *digvijaya* — world-conquest as invoice.

His fleet didn't go the obvious way — through the Malacca choke where Srivijaya waited. They staged at Barus on Sumatra's west coast, slipped south through the Sunda Strait, and hit Palembang from *behind*.

The Tanjavur inscription lists 14 ports sacked: Sri Vijaya, Pannai, Malaiyur (Jambi), Langkasuka (Patani), Takkola (Kra isthmus), Lamuri (Aceh), Nicobar — and finally *Kadaram* — Kedah.

At Kedah and Palembang they tore down the *Vidyadhara-torana* — jewelled war-gates — and carried them to Thanjavur as proof. The captured king Sangrama Vijayotungavarman vanished.

They didn't stay. They left, the strait opened for a century, and Khmer and Javanese kings got rich. Kedah's capital moved from the Muda to the Merbok after the ash cooled.

The Cholas proved Pallava script could be followed by a Tamil sword.
