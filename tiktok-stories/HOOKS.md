> Old: assumed you knew Champa/Borobudur/Kedah. New: only known anchors (Vietnam/Indonesia/Cambodia/India/Malaysia) + rediumvex 100 formulas. Each story has 3 hooks from DIFFERENT categories (Vyral: stop-scroll + retention).

01 Curiosity, 02 Contrarian, 03 Authority, 04 Emotional, 05 Listicle, 06 Question, 07 Story, 08 Negation, 09 Specificity, 10 Confession — we rotate 08/09 (stop) + 07/01 (retain).

## Why Cold Works (Vyral 3-Layer)

- **Visual:** Face closeup (goddess face, stupa lattice, map Sunda vs Malacca, boat ticket closeup, equinox, wall breach, split screen) + movement — human+product +5% VTR, authentic +14%, no outdoor -26%.
- **Verbal:** 9–12 words, negation/question/number, direct-to-camera.
- **Text:** 3–7 words overlay (above), white on dark, top third. Works 85% muted.

## Mix per Video (Example: 01)

- Hook 1 (08 Negation) stops thumb: `Not Buddhist.`
- Hook 2 (01 Curiosity) keeps watch: `Nobody tells you Vietnam has a Hindu temple...`
- Hook 3 (09 Specificity) earns trust: `1,200 years. 2 thefts.`

Batch 3 → test first-3s retention → pick winner. Hooks validate idea (if you can't write cold hook without `Champa`, idea is weak).
