# Hooks That Sell — Cold Audience Fix (Rediumvex + Vyral)

> Old hooks assumed you knew Champa/Borobudur/Kedah. New hooks use only Vietnam/Indonesia/Cambodia/India/Malaysia/Tibet/Bali — known anchors — plus 100 viral formulas.

## The Fix

| Rule | Old (assumes) | New (cold) | Formula |
|------|----------------|------------|---------|
| **No proper nouns in hook** | `Champa`, `Borobudur`, `Kedah` | `Vietnam's beach town`, `Indonesian hill`, `India→Malaysia` | Rediumvex: cold hooks must work muted, no jargon |
| **Negation first** | `This tower...` | `Stop calling... She isn't.` / `Not Vietnamese.` | Cat 08 Negation: 2-word stop-scroll, 0.3s |
| **Odd specific number** | `1,200 years` alone | `1,200-year, 2 thefts, 2,672 panels, 3,000km, 671 ticket` | Cat 09 Specificity: specificity = credibility |
| **Geography violation** | `Champa→Borobudur` | `Vietnam↔Indonesia`, `Tibet←Indonesia` | Pattern interrupt: beach vs temple |

## Vyral 3-Layer Check (All 7 Now Pass)

- **Visual:** Face closeup / movement in first frame (goddess face, drone spiral, map Sunda vs Malacca, Persian ship POV, equinox timelapse, wall breach, split screen). Authentic talking-head +14% (not celebrity -13%).
- **Verbal:** 9–12 words, negation/question/number, spoken direct-to-camera.
- **Text:** 3–7 words overlay (`VIETNAM'S HINDU GODDESS` → `VIETNAM HAS A HINDU TEMPLE`), white on dark, top third.

## Rediumvex 100 Formulas We Leaned On

01 Curiosity (Nobody talks about...), 02 Contrarian (Unpopular opinion, Stop...), 08 Negation (Not X), 09 Specificity (671, 2,672, 3,000km) — we rotated 1 stop-scroll (08/09) + 1 retention (07 Story) per video, per Vyral workflow tip.
