# Hooks That Sell — TikTok / Reels Playbook (Applied to Indianization)

> Synthesis of 46k TikToks (7.3B impressions, Aug 2025) + VidMob + CreatorsJet 200-Reel analysis. Your 7 stories rewritten with the formulas that beat positive framing 1.3–1.8×.

## The Rule of 3s

80% decide in 3s. Strong hook = 34% CTR lift, 60%+ completion; weak = 20-30%; logo intro = 50% drop. Completion > views (Instagram: 4k @80% beats 10k @20%).

## W.A.V.E (Every Story Must Have 4 Layers)

| Layer | What | Keep |
|-------|------|------|
| **W** Words | Text overlay | 3–7 words, top/bottom third, white on dark |
| **A** Audio | First sentence | Negation/question/number, spoken direct-to-camera +14% VTR |
| **V** Visual | First frame | Face closeup, movement, before/after, human+product (+5%), NO static logo |
| **E** Emotion | Trigger | Curiosity gap > direct address; emotional hooks only 3.2% but drive shares |

Celebrity -13% VTR, outdoor -26% (film towers interior/closeup), authentic talking-head wins.

## 5 Formulas That Actually Convert (Use in Rotation)

1. **Contrarian / Unpopular Opinion** — 42-52% hook rate, highest. Template: Stop [common belief]. [Truth]. / Unpopular opinion: X is not Y. → Drives comments (argue).
2. **Stat Shock** — specific odd number + paradox. 2,672 panels. 0 words. → Credibility + reframe.
3. **Curiosity Gap + Urgency** — The [X] they don't want you to know / in 4 days → open loop, must resolve.
4. **Specific Outcome** — I went X→Y in Z / 3,000km for 1 gate → promises proof.
5. **POV / Diagnosis** — POV: your teacher says stay in Sumatra 2 years / Why your Angkor theory fails → identity filter.

Mix: 46k study — optimal length 9 words (news) to 90 (entertainment). For history explainers: 9–12 words audio, 3–5 words text.

## Text Overlay Rules (Sound-Off: 85% Watch Muted)

- Keep to 3-5 words, high contrast, same as audio but shorter.
- Place top third (avoid TikTok UI). Example: PEPPER vs RICE beats Why Bali is Hindu and Java is Muslim (explanation).
- Add subtitles (completion +40%).

## Loop & Payoff

End with callback to hook: Story 01 closes land was female → rewatch opening 'Vietnamese' claim; Story 02 closes you are the sentence → rewatch hidden foot. Loop doubles rewatch rate.
