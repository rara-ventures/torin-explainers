# Sources — By Wave

> Six waves from the Old Pantry to the Industrial Plate. Each folder = `Wave N — Name.md` (story list) + `Wave N — Name — Timeline.md` (dated events).

## Timeline Table

| Wave | Period | Story | Key stamps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **W0 Prelude** | before 1492 | The Old Pantry | wheat westward, sugarcane New Guinea→India, Austronesian banana, all-hot-is-pepper world (timur, pippali, Piper, Sichuan pepper) |
| **W1 Ships & Seeds** | 1493–1550 | First crossings | Columbus 1493 seeds; Gama 1498 → chili at Goa c.1500; tomato Europe 1544 (Fuchs, ornamental) |
| **W2 Galleons & Missions** | 1550–1650 | Two oceans | Manila Galleon (1571 regular): chili/maize/beans Mexico→Asia; cassava Angola; sweet potato Fujian 1590s; cashew/papaya Goa |
| **W3 Peasants' Gardens** | 1650–1800 | Poor adopt first | Prussia 1756, Parmentier 1770s; maize West Africa & Qing highlands; Latini Naples 1692 first tomato-sauce recipe |
| **W4 Naturalization** | 1800–1900 | Citizenship | pizzeria tomato 1830s / Margherita 1889; paprika-Hungary; Irish Famine 1845–52; potato mid-hills Nepal; red kimchi; Sichuan mala; Japan curry 1870s |
| **W5 Industrial Plate** | 1900–now | Brands erase routes | canning→Heinz; frozen pizza 1957; Green Revolution to Nepal 1960s–70s; instant noodles 1971; Hemja Potato Festival 2025 |
