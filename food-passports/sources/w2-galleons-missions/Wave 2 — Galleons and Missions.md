# Wave 2 — Galleons and Missions (1550–1650)

> Two oceans close the loop. The Manila Galleon (Acapulco–Manila, regular from 1571) seeds Pacific Asia with Mexican chilies, maize, beans, squash, pineapple; Portuguese carracks seed the Indian Ocean with Brazil's cassava, papaya, cashew, peanut. Jesuits and merchants are the first botanists: sweet potato into famine-hungry Fujian (1590s), chilies into Chinese herbals as medicine-and-sheat, and by 1650 a farmer in Kerala, Naga, or Kinmen grows American crops he'd never call American.
