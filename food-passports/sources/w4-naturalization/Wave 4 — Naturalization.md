# Wave 4 — Naturalization (1800–1900)

> The century the immigrant becomes the icon. Naples: pizzerias (1830s) then the 1889 Margherita legend — red tomato officially promoted to citizen. Ireland plants potato monocultures until the 1845–52 Famine (the dark mirror of naturalization). Hungary rebrands "Turkish pepper" as paprika; Sichuan's mala duo standardizes; Korea's kimchi turns red; Japan eats British-Indian curry from 1870s. And in the Himalaya: potato crosses into Nepal's mid-hills via British-India hill stations and portage routes — Ghalu, Rolpa origin stories — *verify* — a settlement so complete it produced a **National Potato Festival** in Hemja 2025.
