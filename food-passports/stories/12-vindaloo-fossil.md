# S12: Vindaloo is a pickling instruction, not a dish

**The assumption:** Vindaloo = the hottest "Indian" curry (UK takeaway canon).

**The reveal:** The word is **Portuguese *vinho d'alho* — wine and garlic**, a preservation marinade (goan Catholic *vimto*/vindaloo). The Goan version used palm vinegar + Kashmiri-style mild chilies; the British-era takeaway **fire version** is a 20c. restaurant invention outside India ⚠. So the dish is: a Portuguese word + Indian vinegar logic + an Amazonian chili + a UK restaurant rewilding. Nobody "owns" it; the **word itself is Door 1's fossil** — Estado da Índia sealed in a curry name.

**Kicker:** Order vindaloo and you're pronouncing sixteenth-century Lisbon — in a bowl of Brazil, cooked in Goa, made dangerous in Birmingham.

**Verify:** Goan vs Bengali *jhingal/Bindaloo* records, the UK hotter-version dating.
