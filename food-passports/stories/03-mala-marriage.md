# S3: Sichuan's soul is a 300-year-old marriage

**The assumption:** Sichuan = numbing-hot, ancient Chinese heat.

**The reveal:** The *ma* (numbing) is native — Sichuan pepper, used for 2,000+ years, one of the "five flavors" of ancient China. The *la* (hot) is an immigrant — Capsicum from the Amazon, in Sichuan only since ~1700s (first records in gazetteers ⚠ 1749–55). *Mala* — the signature — is a **mixed marriage with a marriage certificate 300 years old**. Before it: Sichuan heat = pepper, mustard, ginger, cornelian cherries. Mapo tofu, the "classic," is a **1860s** invention ⚠.

**Proof stamps:** 1591 first Chinese capsicum text ⚠ attribution → coastal spread → Qing highland adoption → 1749/55 Sichuan gazetteers → c.1860s mapo tofu → doubanjiang becomes orthodoxy.

**Kicker:** When chili arrived, Sichuan didn't replace its flavor — it married the new to the old and invented a "tradition" so convincing the world now thinks China discovered spiciness in a Mexican plant.
