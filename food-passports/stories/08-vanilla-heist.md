# S8: The flower that refused to seed outside Mexico — until a 12-year-old played the bee

**The assumption:** Vanilla = flavor; Madagascar = "vanilla country."

**The reveal:** Vanilla orchid (Veracruz, Totonac garden) sets seed only with one Mexican bee (*Melipona*). Europe imported the vine and got **nothing** for 300 years — the bee never crossed. In 1841 ⚠ Réunion, an enslaved/adopted 12-year-old (Edmond Albius ⚠ contested biography) worked out hand-pollination with a stick; France moved the technique to Madagascar/Comoros and broke Mexico's monopoly within decades. Today Madagascar + Indonesia supply ~90% of natural vanilla and eat almost none of it — ice cream in Paris does.

**Kicker:** A flavor, a stolen technique, an orphaned bee, a child, and a plantation geography that still feeds Europe — everything your "ice cream ingredient" hides.

**Verify before air:** Albius's story is famous but heavily mythologized; Melipona-only claim is overstated (hummingbirds too). Run research prompt.
