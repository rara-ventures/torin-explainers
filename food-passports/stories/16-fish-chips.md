# S16: Fish and chips — England's soul is a Jewish immigrant's pan and a French potato

**The assumption:** since Shakespeare, England = fish & chips.

**The reveal:** fried fish "in the Jewish fashion" entered London with **Sephardic immigrants** (16th–17th c., refugees of the Iberian expulsions — the same 1492 that sailed Columbus ⚠ link); chips are the French/Belgian potato's northward walk (S07's tuber). The combo = ~1860s-70s (Malin's shop claim ⚠). Its "eternal" status sealed in **WWII**: one of the few un-rationed dishes — the Blitz paired it with bulldog spirit and it never left the menu of memory.

**Proof stamps:** 1492 (both fleets) ⚠ → 1700s "fried fish, Jewish fashion" in English print ⚠ → 1860s first shops ⚠ → 1940 rationing exception → 1970s "British cuisine" shorthand.

**Kicker:** the two ships of 1492 — Columbus's and the expelled Jews' — meet in a chippy. And when Thatcher-era critics said the dish was "unhealthy foreign stuff"… the fish was already an immigrant.
