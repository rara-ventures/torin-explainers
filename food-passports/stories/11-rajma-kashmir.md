# S11: Rajma-chawal is only ~150 years old (and the bean is American)

**The assumption:** Rajma = Dogra/Pandit staple "since time immemorial," identity food.

**The reveal:** Kidney beans = *Phaseolus vulgaris*, New World (Mesoamerica/Andes). Old India had mung, urad, pigeon pea, cowpea — zero kidney beans. The bean crossed to Kashmir likely via **Portuguese/gateway routes through Lahore or Lhasa-side trade in the 1800s** ⚠ (local histories say "introduced ~100–150 yrs ago"; weak sources). And *rajma-maach* is now *the* Kashmiri weekend statement, eaten with the same pride any "ancient" dish carries.

**Kicker:** If your grandmother's grandmother in Jammu had asked for rajma, no one would have known the word — and it's already the most loyal citizen in the whole vault: the bean took Kashmiri nationality without ever a newspaper noticing.

**Nepal crossover:** *simi* — same bean, same century, same story, in your own dal-bhat rotation.

**Verify:** this is the weakest documented story in the vault (and therefore potentially the most original — research prompt priority 1).
