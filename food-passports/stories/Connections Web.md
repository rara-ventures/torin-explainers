# Connections Web — chains that make one story feed the next

> Each chain = 3+ S-tier cards; run them as multi-part episodes, or close any single card by pointing at the next link. This is the vault's differentiator: facts connected, not listed.

## C1 · 1492, both fleets — the "two ships" chain
[[16-fish-chips]] · [[01-tomato-italy]] · [[21-samosa-sambosa]] · [[18-tea-stolen]]
Columbus's pepper and the Sephardic expulsion leave Iberia the same year; four centuries later they meet in a London chip shop. Every "national" dish of a receiving country holds at least one stamp from 1492±.

## C2 · The chili's three capitals
[[12-vindaloo-fossil]] → [[03-mala-marriage]] → [[04-paprika-turkish]] → [[02-kimchi-was-white]] + Nepal flank: see line above
One shrub, four capitals of "spice = identity": Goa, Chengdu, Budapest, Seoul. The proof it's one plant: the names still say *foreign* (Turkish pepper, "barbarian pepper", kara-). Nepal's side of the same war — timur vs chiuri — lives in [[06-ugali-mexico]]'s grain twin and [[33-kodo-reversal]].

## C3 · War rations → family heritage
[[14-carbonara-1944]] · [[28-spam-musubi]] · [[29-thanksgiving-1863]] · [[15-general-tso-fortune]]
Armies move food faster than merchants: US bacon-and-eggs → carbonara; base surplus → budae-jjigae & musubi; canning + magazine + president → Thanksgiving; exclusion-era adaptation → General Tso. All four are ≤180 years old and defended as eternal.

## C4 · The state brands the plate
[[31-bacalhau-salazar]] · [[27-feijoada-myth]] · [[20-espresso-1901]] · [[17-tikka-masala]]
Dictatorships, monarchists, patents, and a parliament speech: governments *finish* naturalizations. Portugal imported a myth; Brazil imported a story about its myth; Italy trademarked a machine; Britain crowned a Bangladeshi-made curry "national."
