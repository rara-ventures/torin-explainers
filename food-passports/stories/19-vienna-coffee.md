# S19: Vienna's coffee houses run on the Ottoman army's abandoned sacks

**The assumption:** coffee-house culture = Viennese heritage since forever (UNESCO-listed!).

**The reveal:** coffee reached Vienna **with the Ottomans** — the 1683 failed siege legend says sacks of strange beans were left behind, and a Ukrainian/Armenian intermediary (Kolschitzky/Pierre claims ⚠ contested) taught Vienna to roast and sweet them. First privilege ~1683–85 ⚠. Vienna then did what every receiving kitchen does: added milk, sugar (S18's commodity) and cake, wrote the etiquette — and 300 years later the *Zuckerbäcker* coffee-house reads more "Habsburg" than the croissant it's served with (which also claims the same 1683 siege as its origin legend ⚠).

**Proof stamps:** 1400s Yemen sufis → 1500s Istanbul coffeehouses → 1683 siege + sacks legend ⚠ → 1685 first Viennese privilege ⚠ → 2003 UNESCO coffee-house culture.

**Kicker:** the calmest "European" ritual in the world is a souvenir from history's most feared army — same mechanism as paprika's "Turkish pepper" (S04): the enemy's ingredient, laundered through three generations, becomes the host's essence.
