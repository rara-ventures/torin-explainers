# S7: The Andean tuber with a Nepali party — the full-circle story

**The assumption:** "Aloo" is Nepali food — the tarkari half of dal-bhat, aachal, waiwansh life.

**The reveal:** Potato: domesticated around Lake Titicaca 6,000+ years ago, freeze-dried as *ch'uñu* by Tiwanaku, carried by Spaniards to Europe (1560s, feared/animal-feed), then into South Asia via Portuguese Goa and British-India hill-station agriculture — into Nepal's mid-hills roughly **1800s** ⚠ (Rolpa/Ghalu origin traditions). Green Revolution seed programs in the 1960s–70s made it a hill cash crop. Then the closer with the photo: **April 2025, National Potato Festival, Hemja, Kaski — a 50,000 kg potato pile, 550 ha under cultivation, farmers celebrating an Andean tuber as local heritage.** [source](https://en.sicomedia.com/2025/0407/47484.shtml)

**Kicker:** The half of dal-bhat your great-great-grandparents never met now has a festival, a highway load, and a price you complain about in Nepali. Full circle: Andes → ship → Goa → Rolpa → parade.
