# S2: Kimchi used to be white

**The assumption:** Red kimchi = Korea, since forever. UNESCO-level "eternal tradition."

**The reveal:** Joseon-era kimchi was salt-fermented and **white** — sometimes mustard-seed spiced. Chilies reached Korea via Japan (Tsushima trade) or China in the 1600s and only became the default kimchi ingredient in the **18th–19th centuries** ⚠. A subject of King Jeongjo would not recognize today's national dish.

**Proof stamps:** 1493 Columbus "pepper" → 1542 Nanban open → 1600s Korean records of chili as medicine/curiosity ⚠ → 1800s red kimchi standardizes → 1900s gochugaru industry → 2013 kimjang UNESCO.

**Kicker:** Korea defended kimchi against Japan's attempts to make it "global" — while its core ingredient was originally from the Americas. Tradition isn't a bloodline. It's a marriage certificate.
