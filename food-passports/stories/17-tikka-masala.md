# S17: "Britain's national dish" was invented in Britain, by Bangladeshis

**The assumption:** chicken tikka masala = India's gift; and Indian restaurants = India.

**The reveal:** "Indian restaurant" in Britain is overwhelmingly **Bangladeshi (Sylheti)** — post-1971 migration pipeline (⚠ 80–90% claim to verify). Tikka masala's gravy — creamed, tomato-sauced tandoor chunks — is a **pan-Britain invention**: Glasgow's Ali Ahmed 1971 claim vs "already in Punjab" claims (⚠). Robin Cook in 2001 declared it Britain's true national dish. And "curry" as a category? an imperial filing word, *turu murri* flattened into one colonial heading.

**Proof stamps:** 1700s "curry" in British recipes (Hannah Glasse!) → 1971 Glasgow claim → 2001 Cook speech → 2009 "curry is a colonial word" restaurant rebrands.
