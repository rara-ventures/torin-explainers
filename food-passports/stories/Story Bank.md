# Story Bank — ranked by the gasp

> Scoring: **assumption depth** (how universally the wrong belief is held) × **reveal surprise** × **proof quality**. One card per story. Green-light tiers run as scripts; ⚠ cards need the verify pass before airing.

| Tier | Story | Assumption people die on | Reveal in one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥇 S | [[01-tomato-italy]] | "Italy = tomatoes since Rome" | Tomatoes were ornamentals in Italy until the 1600s; "Roman" food never tasted one |
| 🥇 S | [[02-kimchi-was-white]] | "Koreans have eaten red kimchi forever" | Kimchi was white; gochugaru is a 400-year-old immigrant |
| 🥇 S | [[03-mala-marriage]] | "Sichuan has always been hot" | *Mala* = native peppercorn married an Amazonian chili in the 1700s |
| 🥇 S | [[04-paprika-turkish]] | "Paprika = Hungary" | Its own Hungarian name blames Turkey; Turkey got it from Columbus |
| 🥈 A | [[05-grano-turco]] | "Polenta is ancient Veneto" | Even the Italian name for corn misreads the passport — *grano turco* |
| 🥈 A | [[06-ugali-mexico]] | "Ugali is African grain" | Kenya's word for food is a Mexican grass; sorghum survived only in beer |
| 🥈 A | [[07-potato-festival]] | "Potato is Nepali identity food" | Andean tuber → Goa → British India → Hemja festival, 200 years end-to-end |
| 🥈 A | ⚠ [[08-vanilla-heist]] | "Madagascar vanilla" | The vine can't make beans outside Mexico without a bee that never left — until a 12-year-old learned to play bee |
| 🥈 A | ⚠ [[09-chocolate-paradox]] | "African chocolate country" | Africa grows most of the world's cacao and barely eats any |
| 🥉 B | [[10-ketchup-china]] | "Ketchup is American" | The word is a Chinese fish-sauce label; America put a tomato in it |
| 🥉 B | ⚠ [[11-rajma-kashmir]] | "Rajma-chawal is timeless" | Kidney beans are New World; Kashmir got them ~150–200 years ago |
