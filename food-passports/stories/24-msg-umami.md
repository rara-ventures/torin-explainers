# S24: MSG — the "Chinese cheat chemical" was extracted from Japanese dashi, and China now makes the world's supply

A century of Japanese industry sells the "fifth taste" as high science — then the world's biggest MSG producer becomes **China**, feeding the processed-food systems of Africa and Asia (Nigeria's indomie-and-suya seasoning economy ⚠). Japan's most ancient flavor claim (dashi) became its most modern export (crystal); the West's "foreign cheat" is a Tokyo university paper.
