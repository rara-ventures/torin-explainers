# Kitchen: Southeast Asia (Thai · Filipino · Indonesian · Vietnamese)

Both doors land here. **Manila door**: Mexican chilies/maize/beans → Bicol (Naga = Asia's Mexican palate), Visayas; galleon-era maize becomes Ifugao? — *NO: Ifugao rice stayed; maize entered lowlands (verify)*. **Goa/Malacca door**: chili, papaya, pineapple, cassava, cashew into Malay/Thai/Vietnamese kitchens. Result: "ancient" SE Asian heat (rhizomes, Piper, fermented sour) reorganized around capsicum in ~150 yrs. Peanuts → *satay* & *gado-gado*; cassava → *keripik*, Thai starch industry; pineapple → national fruit brands (Philippines was top exporter — *verify* era).

- Rice (ancient ✔✔), coconut, banana/plantain (pre-Columbian ✔), taro, yam, lime (via Arab/Indian Ocean trade — *verify*), betel; heat = ginger/rhizomes, Piper, torch ginger; sour-ferments (belacan, *paek*-family)
