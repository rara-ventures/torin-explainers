# Kitchen: East Asia (Sichuan · Korea · Japan · Fujian)

Three doors, three cuisines rewritten. **China**: chilies via southeast coast (1591 herbal), then highland maize & sweet potato feeding the Qing population boom — the exchange as *demographic infrastructure*. **Korea**: chilies via Tsushima/China, kimchi turning red late (18th–19c. *verify*). **Japan**: Nanban imports (chili, then *kabocha* via Meiji-era American seed 1880s–90s *verify*), sweet potato = *satsumaimo*, peanut = *nankin-mame* ("Nanking bean" — a route label in a name), curry from the British navy 1870s.

## Pre-immigrant pantry
- Rice, wheat (north), millet, soy (all ancient ✔✔); heat = Sichuan pepper, mustard, ginger, zhuyu cornel; Japan: yuzu, sansho, wasabi, karashi
- Korea: white salt-ferments, native alliums; no red anything
