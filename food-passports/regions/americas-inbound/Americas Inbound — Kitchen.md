# Kitchen: Americas — the Reverse Immigration

The lab's balance of payments. Old World crops naturalized **in** the Americas so hard they became national exports with zero foreign accent: wheat → Argentine pasta culture & the US bread basket (Italian-Argentine *pasta* 19–20c.); sugarcane → Brazil cachaça & Caribbean rum (the plantation's drink identity); coffee → Brazil 1727→national product, Colombia 1800s→**the** brand (Juan Valdez 1959 — *verify*); banana → Central America became "banana" itself (the term outgrew the fruit's geography; Ecuador world #1 exporter — *verify*); citrus → Valencia oranges *in* Florida & São Paulo; cattle/dairy → "Argentine beef" is a post-Columbian animal on grass that used to host bison. **Every "product of the Americas" gets checked twice: pre-Columbian or colonial transplant?**
