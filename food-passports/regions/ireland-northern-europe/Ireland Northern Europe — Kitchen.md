# Kitchen: Ireland & Northern Europe

The cautionary naturalization. Potato enters 16c. as garden exotic, is *pushed* by states (Prussia 1756 era) and *taken* by peasants for one reason: calories per acre beyond anything medieval agriculture had. Ireland bets the population on it (1700s–40s — *verify* acreage stats), 1845–52 collects the debt (Famine: 1M dead, 1–2M emigrated — *verify ranges*); Germany/Russia's potato belt feeds industrialization — *Kartoffel* as Prussian discipline cliché by 1900. Maize's role here is animal & industrial (W5). **Citizenship so total it became a single-clone dependency — the lab's proof that naturalization is real when the crop is load-bearing.**

## Pre-immigrant pantry
- Grains: rye, barley, oats (Ireland's actual ancient star), wheat; pottage of peas/beans; dairy-heavy Irish protein
- No capsicum (except garden), no tuber except turnip & parsnip
