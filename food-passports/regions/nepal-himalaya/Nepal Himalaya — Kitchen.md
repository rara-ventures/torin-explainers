# Kitchen: Nepal & Himalaya

**The lab's home kitchen** — and its cleanest demonstration. Two centuries ago the mid-hills plate was: bhat (rice, ancient ✔), gahat/kaguno dal (local legumes ✔), saag (greens ✔), bitter gourd and mango pickles, meat — and ** timur + ji-tima heat**. Today's plate: *aloo* in every tarkari, chiuri in every achar, tomato-based achhar, sanako/simi beans, and a potato festival in Kaski. Same word — *tarkari* — new continent.

## Pre-immigrant pantry (W0 baseline)
- Rice, foxtail & little millet, buckwheat (fapar), finger millet (kodo), taro, yam Dioscorea, banana (pre-Columbian, ancient ✔)
- Heat: timur (Zanthoxylum), ji-tima (Torenia/"Nepolese pepper"), wild pepper, mustard, ginger, garlic
- Fats: ghee, mustard oil; pickles: oleaster (nipro), mango, lemon
