# Dish: Tom Yum Goong (Thailand)

**The receipt:** the *aromatic* half is ancient Thai — lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime, fish sauce; the *hot* half is **bird's-eye chili *prik kee noo* — Capsicum frutescens, Amazonian**, arrived via Portuguese/Manila routes 16–17c.; lime is Old World ✔ (via Arab/Indian Ocean trade — *verify*); curry-paste cookery was rewritten in ~150 years. "Thai = spicy" is a post-1500 fact: pre-chili Thai heat was black-pepper and fermented sour — the original *som tam* used pepper, not chili, and green papaya itself arrived later (*verify*).

**Script hook:** "Thailand's five flavors got their 'hot' from an Amazonian shrub the Portuguese dropped at Ayutthaya."
