# Dish: Bicol Express (Naga City, c. 1960s)

**The receipt:** coconut milk (Pacific ✔ ancient), pork ✔, **red chili — from Mexico by Manila Galleon (1565–1815)**. The Philippines is Asia's most direct heir of the *Pacific* chili route: Naga's sili cuisine sits at the Acapulco terminus of the trans-Pacific chile/maize/tomato/avocado shuttle. Named after the *Bicol Express* bus line (1960s — *verify*) — a dish that named itself after a vehicle, then became "the" regional identity. Pre-colonial Bicolano heat: probably none above ginger-level; the "fiery Philippine cuisine" is 400 years old.

**Script hook:** "A Mexican pepper, a Pacific coconut, and a bus line — that's Bicol Express, and that's the Philippines' signature heat."
