# Dish: Jollof Rice (Senegambia)

**The receipt:** rice — **and Africa has its own rice** (*Oryza glaberrima*, domesticated in the Niger delta independently, ~1000 BCE ✔✔) plus Asian rice arriving via Indian Ocean & colonial routes; the tomato-onion-pepper base — tomato is New World (Portuguese-era introduction, *verify Senegambia dating*), Scotch bonnet chili — Amazonian, named "scotch" by nobody knowing why; sugar (via plantation world). Wolof *thiéboudienne* is the ancestor; "jollof wars" are the citizenship exam between Nigeria and Ghana — **two countries fighting over a dish whose key ingredients are all immigrants**, while the rice question is genuinely, locally old.
