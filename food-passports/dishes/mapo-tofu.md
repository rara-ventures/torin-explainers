# Dish: Mapo Tofu (Chengdu, c. 1860s)

**The receipt:** tofu (Han-dynasty local ✔), doubanjiang (Pixian — fermented chilis: **New World + Old World fermentation tech**, chili-in-China post-1500s ✔), Sichuan pepper (the original *ma*, local ✔✔), beef/pork, *douchi*. The dish's entire signature — *málà* — is a **merger**: Sichuan pepper's native numbness married to a 16th-c. Amazonian burn that entered Sichuan through gazetteer-era adoption (*first record 1749/1755 — verify*). Before the merger, pre-chili Sichuan heat was: pepper, mustard seed, ginger, cornel (zhuyu). Old Sichuan would not recognize this dish; new Sichuan can't eat without it.

**Script hook:** "Sichuan's soul — *mala* — is a marriage certificate between a 2,000-year-old peppercorn and a 400-year-old immigrant."
