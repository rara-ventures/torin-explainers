# Dish: Kimchi

**The receipt:** napa cabbage (local ✔ — the one honest ingredient), salt, garlic, ginger (old recipe core), and **gochugaru — chili powder from the Americas**, arriving Korea via two contested routes: Nanban Japan (Tsushima trade, post-1542) or overland via Ming/Qing China (*verify — scholarship favors Japan-by-1600s, adoption slow; red kimchi standardizes only 18th–19th c.*). Joseon's kimchi was **white** — salt-fermented, sometimes mustard-seed-spiced. A grandmother from 1700 Seoul would consider today's kimchi a novelty. Winter-kimchi communal making (kimjang, UNESCO 2013) ritualized an immigrant.

**Script hook:** "For most of its history kimchi was white. Korea's most guarded tradition is 400 years old and Mexican-adjacent."
