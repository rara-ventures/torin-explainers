# Dish: Chicken Paprikás (Hungary)

**The receipt:** chicken + sour cream + onion (local trio ✔✔✔) + **paprika** — a New World chili that entered 16th-c. Hungary as an Ottoman curiosity called * Turkish pepper*, grown by Serbian gardeners (Bácska), and rebranded *hungary paprika / Kalocsa-Szeged* as national essence by the 19th c. (Gundel cookbooks era — *verify first paprika-in-recipe dating: 1775? Nikolaus Pākai?*). The great irony: **the spice Europe calls "Hungarian" displaced the black pepper and caraway it once used**, and its name in most languages still says *Turk* (Törökpaprika → "Turkish pepper").

**Script hook:** "Hungary sued the world over 'real' paprika — over a pepper whose own Hungarian name blames Turkey. The pepper itself blames the Americas."
