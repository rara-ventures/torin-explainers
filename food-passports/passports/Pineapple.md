# Passport: Pineapple

**Born:** Guaraní/Tupi gardens. Columbus tasted it in Guadeloupe 1493 and called it *pomme de pin*. **Passport says:** South America. **Flags fly (as export brand):** Hawaii (Dole, "the pineapple island" — a US state famous for a South American fruit), Philippines, Thailand, Costa Rica. In SE Asia it naturalized into actual cuisine (*ginata*-style Filipino desserts, Thai sour curries, Malay *nanas* in *rendang*-adjacent cook) — citizenship in some kitchens, pure plantation brand in others. Bangladesh's *anaras* is now a luxury-fruit identity crop (*verify* colonial-era introduction routes).
