# Passport: Potato

**Born:** around Lake Titicaca. **Passport says:** Peru. **Flags fly:** Ireland 🇮🇪, Nepal 🇳🇵, Germany 🇩🇪, Russia, Belarus — the crop that fed (and starved) the modern North, and rewrites Himalayan agriculture. Feared as cattle feed and *suspected of causing leprosy* before peasants proved kings wrong.
