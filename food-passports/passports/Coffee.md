# Passport: Coffee

**Born:** Ethiopia; institutionalized in Yemen 1400s, Cairo/Istanbul coffeehouses 1500s. Then the fastest identity-swap on record: Dutch seedlings → Java 1696 → French/Gabriel de Clieu's famous single plant → Martinique 1720 (legend — *verify*) → Brazil 1727 → Colombia 1800s. **Two centuries** between "unknown New World shrub" and "Colombia = coffee" nation-brand (Juan Valdez, 1959 — *verify*). Vietnam became world #2 producer in 40 years (French colonial planting + Đổi Mới rush) with a domestic *cà phê sữa đá* culture — a rare case of an industrial crop *also* getting citizenship. The bean never grew where it's drunk; the drink never stayed where it grew.
