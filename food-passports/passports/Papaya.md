# Passport: Papaya

**Born:** Maya lowlands. **Passport says:** Mexico. **Flags fly:** Goa ("pawta" — as local as coconut), Bangladesh (*rajbhog* varieties bred to national-icon status), Hawaii ("papaya" brand), Thailand (*som tam* — the green-papaya salad is a *modern* import, 20th c. — verify: *som tam* with green papaya standardizes mid-1900s). Its name is the most honest on record: *popaw* → Taino → Spanish → everywhere. Raw papaya *achar* in Nepali and Bengali kitchens is a Columbian-Exchange side dish wearing no foreign accent.
