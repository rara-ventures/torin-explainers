# Passport: Banana

**Born:** Malay world/New Guinea coasts; Austronesian ships carried it west — the *pre*-Columbian triumph (plantain cultures of Uganda and Tanzania by the first millennium, per Pilcher/Henry). Portuguese added it to the Atlantic: Santo Domingo 1516 (Friar Berlanga — *verify*); West Africa's plantain economy deepens; and then the 19th century **inverts the fruit**: the Gros Michel, then Cavendish, become the industrial monoculture of Latin America — "banana republic" (1901 United Fruit, *verify coinage*) as the political term a food coined. Uganda's *matooke* may be the world's most thorough naturalization: a SE Asian fruit that became the national starch of an East African kingdom.
