# Passport: Wheat — the reverse immigrant

**Born:** Fertile Crescent. Old World veteran of the Silk Roads (noodles-in-China debates, pasta-in-Mediterranean debates — the *pre*-1492 chapter our W0 vector owns). After 1492 it crossed **west**: Columbus seeded Hispaniola 1493; by the 1800s the Pampas and Great Plains were feeding Europe — and by the 1900s, Argentina was a pasta nation and the US a bread-basket exporter. Modern stamp no one reads: **Italy imports a large share of its durum** (Canada, US) — *verify current figures* — so the "Italian" wheat in Italian pasta holds an American-Canadian passport. Wheat is the control crop proving the thesis runs **both directions**.
