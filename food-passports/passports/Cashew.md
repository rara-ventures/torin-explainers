# Passport: Cashew

**Born:** Brazilian coasts. Portuguese carried it east **as a garden curiosity and soil-stabilizer** — 1560s Goa — and nobody told India it was Brazilian. Today India is the world's largest cashew *processor/eater*; Goa's "fruits of Goa" identity; Vietnamese *bò bía*-adjacent snack culture and Brazil's own re-export irony (Africa grows, India processes, world eats). Cashew-chicken curries and *kaju barfi* read as ancient; *kaju* even appears in "Mughlai" menus that predate... don't. *Verify:* first records of cashew in Indian cookery (19th c.? Goan Portuguese *doce* use first?).
