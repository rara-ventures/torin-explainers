# Passport: Peanut

**Born:** Peru/Brazil. **Passport says:** South America. **Flags fly:** West Africa, Indonesia, Thailand, Madagascar. The classic case of a crop diffusing faster than documents: Portuguese ships moved it from Brazil to the Guinea coast and Asia within ~50 years of 1500; by the 1800s the "groundnut belt" was an African geographic fact, and Yoruba *groundnut stew* and Senegambian *mafé*-kin sauces read as ancient. Satay's peanut sauce in Java rides the same Manila/Acapulco-and-Goa circuit. Its names are maps: *groundnut*, *pixi*, *arachide*, *mandai*, *kalung* — each a route.
