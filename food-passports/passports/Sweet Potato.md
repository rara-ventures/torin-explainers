# Passport: Sweet Potato

**Born:** tropical America — and yet it may have touched the Pacific *before* Columbus (Kon-Tiki question; Triton's trumpet evidence in Polynesia c. 1000–1100 CE — *verify*). The documented route: Spain → Philippines/Liuqiu → Fujian, 1590s. A merchant's son smuggled vines out of Manila during trade bans; a provincial governor memorialized the throne in 1594 to plant it against famine. It became the calorie engine of China's Qing population boom and Japan's *satsumaimo*.
