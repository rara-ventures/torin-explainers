# Passport: Cacao

**Born:** Amazon; made famous by Maya and Mexica as frothed, spiced, unsweetened *xocolatl*. **Passport says:** Mexico. **Flags fly (as labor, not cuisine):** Côte d'Ivoire + Ghana grow ~60% of world beans; Switzerland/Belgium own the brand. Cacao is the lab's defining **transit-labor crop**: Africa took the plantation, not the plate. Europe added the two things the crop never had — sugar (itself a reverse immigrant, see [[Sugarcane]]) and milk (Eagle 1875 — *verify*). Confectionery's "luxury of the Aztecs" marketing is 1840s–now brand-building on a real tomb.
