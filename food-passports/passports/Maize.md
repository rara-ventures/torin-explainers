# Passport: Maize

**Born:** Mexico, from teosinte — a grass no wild analog resembles. **Passport says:** Mexico. **Flags fly:** Veneto (polenta), Kenya/Tanzania (ugali), West Africa (ogi), Qing highlands. The crop that converted more land than any empire — and whose Italian name, *grano turco* ("Turkish grain"), misreads the passport twice.

## Stamps
| When | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1493 | Hispaniola | Columbus; spread via Islamic trade routes (hence "Turkish") |
| 1500s–1600s | Iberia, N Italy | poenta of the Venetian poor replaces chestnut & farro |
| 1550s–1600s | West Africa | via Portuguese; maize + groundnut pairing transforms protein |
| 1570s–1700s | China | coast → Qing highlands; fuel for the 18th-c. population boom (*verify* dates) |
| 19c. | East Africa | Swahili coast → ugali standardizes |
| 1960s– | Global | Green Revolution hybrids; CIMMYT → Nepal's Terai |

## Citizenship test
Unmarked use ✔ (ugali *is* food in Swahili), branding ✔ (polenta as Veneto identity), displacement ✔ (sorghum/millet/purslane → displaced on hills; chestnut → displaced in Veneto), festival ✗.
