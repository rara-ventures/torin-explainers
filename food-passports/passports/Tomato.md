# Passport: Tomato

**Born:** Andes, cultivated by Aztecs. **Passport says:** Mexico. **Flag flies:** Italy. Europe grew it as an ornamental "golden apple" for 200 years before Neapolitan poor put it in a pan — then the nation built an identity on it.
