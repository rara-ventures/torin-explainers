# Passport: Sugarcane

**Born:** New Guinea; made a technology in India (crystallization, *khanda* → "candy"). The arc Mintz mapped: carried west by Islamic agriculture (Egypt, Sicily, Madeira), then Columbus 1493 planted it in the Americas — where it became **the first industrial food system**: mill + boiling house + enslaved labor + plantation geography, sugar → rum → slaves → molasses triangle. Brazil 1500s–1600s, Caribbean 1640s+, then beet sugar (1800s Europe) broke the monopoly but not the habit. Cane-fed fermentation gave the New World its drinks: cachaça, rum, and the planter economy that moved more food-people than every crop on this list combined. `direction: inbound` marks the *New World settlement* — the crop's true home is the Old.
