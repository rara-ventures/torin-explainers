# Passport: Chili Pepper

**Born:** Amazonia/Mesoamerica. **Passport says:** six countries at once — the most successfully naturalized crop in history. It entered Asia through **two doors**: the Goa door (Portuguese, c. 1500) and the Manila door (Galleon, 1571+), then outran its own name. Wherever it landed it murdered the local pepper trade: Piper in Kerala, timur/ji-tima in Nepal, long pepper in Bengal, Sichuan pepper's solo act in China (saved only as the *ma* half of *mala*).
