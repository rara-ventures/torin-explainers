# Passport: Pumpkin & Squash

**Born:** Mexico (pepo), Andes/North America (maxima — the big orange ones), Central America (moschata — butternut). **Passport says:** Americas ×3. **Flags fly:** Japan (*kabocha* — sold as "American pumpkin" in Meiji-era seed packets, now a vegetable with 300 cultivars), Korea (*hobak-jeon*, "pumpkin oil-pan-fry", festival food), India (*kaddu*), Thailand. A permanent guest — adopted everywhere, claimed nowhere. Its reverse migration is charming: the "Italian" zucchini is a New World squash domesticated in 1900s Italy.
