# Passport: Common Bean

**Born:** two independent gene pools — Mesoamerica and the Andes. **Passport says:** Americas. **Flags fly:** Kashmir (*rajma-chawal*, arguably the most "native" lunch on earth), Italy (pasta e fagioli, borlotti, cassoulet's *lingot* in France), Nepal (*simi/sanako* as modern staple). Old World beans (*Vigna*, *Cajanus*, chickpea) existed; the common bean conquered dry-bean cookery wholesale from Punjab to Tuscany within ~250 years.
