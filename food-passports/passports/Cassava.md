# Passport: Cassava

**Born:** Brazil. **Passport says:** Brazil. **Flags fly:** Congo, Nigeria, Kerala, Thailand. The Portuguese carried it to Africa on the same ships that carried people — and it repaid the cruelty by out-yielding every local root on poor soil, feeding forest frontiers that no state controlled. Today the DRC grows more cassava than any crop; Kerala's *appa* and *kappa* run on it; Thai starch feeds the instant-noodle world. Cyanide and all — the bitter varieties need soaking, a technology African and Indian cooks inherited from Brazilians.
