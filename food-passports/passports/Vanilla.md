# Passport: Vanilla

**Born:** Veracruz, pollinated by one endemic orchid bee (*Melipona*). **Passport says:** Mexico. **Flags fly:** Madagascar + Indonesia (90%+ of natural vanilla today) — but not as flavor heritage, as export crop. The great heist: hand-pollination technique (1841, Albius, Réunion, age 12? — *verify legend*) let France ship the orchid to Indian Ocean islands; Mexico lost its monopoly in decades. Madagascar's vanilla belongs to ice cream in Paris; no Malagasy dessert tradition claims it. Purest **transit labor** passport in the vault — a stolen technique, a grown-for-export bean, zero citizenship.
