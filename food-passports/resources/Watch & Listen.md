# Watch & Listen — Food Passports media index

> Books first (this field is book-rich), video second. Everything marked *verify* must be read/watched before a note's claim becomes `status: verified`.

## Core shelf (orientation)
- Alfred Crosby, **The Columbian Exchange** (1972) — the term; re-read ch. on food & demography.
- Charles C. Mann, **1493** (2011) — the exchange's human story; ColumbianExchange.org companion site.
- Rachel Laudan, **Cuisine and Empire** (2013) — "culinary transfers" framing; use her grid for W4–W5.
- Sidney Mintz, **Sweetness and Power** (1985) — sugar = the plantation vector in one book.
- E. N. Anderson, **The Food of China** (1988) — New World crops & Qing demography (W2–W4).
- Michael Pilcher, **Food in World History** (2006) — diaspora/industrial legs (W4–W5).
- Ken Albala (ed.), **Food: A Cultural Culinary History** (Great Courses, 2013) — video lecture survey.
- Video: Great Courses food surveys (Albala above); PBS/Smithsonian corn & potato features (*verify titles before citing in scripts — no unverified titles on air*).
