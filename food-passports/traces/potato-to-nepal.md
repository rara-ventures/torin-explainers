# Trace: Potato → Nepal (Andes to Hemja)

## Route
**Lake Titicaca (W0, ch'uñu) → Seville (W1: 1560s) → Goa via Portuguese (W1–W2) → Company-era India & hill stations → Nepal mid-hills, c. early-mid 1800s (Ghalu, Rolpa origin traditions — *verify via research prompt: Kirkpatrick 1793 Kathmandu sighting?*) → Green Revolution seed programs (W5) → **National Potato Festival, Hemja, Kaski, April 2025: 50,000 kg pile; 550 ha under potato in one pocket of the Annapurna foothills** (source: [SATV/Rising Nepal](https://en.sicomedia.com/2025/0407/47484.shtml)).**

## Why the mid-hills?
Potato likes what the Himalaya has: cool slopes, rainfall, soils where rice fails above ~2,000 m. It **completed the *tarkari* half of dal-bhat** and turned hill farms into market gardens. The crop that Irish history warns about became Nepali food security — different scale, different state, same plant.
