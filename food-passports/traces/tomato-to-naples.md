# Trace: Tomato → Naples → "Italy"

## Route (one crop, four centuries)
**Andes → Tenochtitlan market (W0) → Seville as botanical oddity (W1: 1544 Fuchs) → Naples *pomo d'oro* gardens (W2) → Latini prints sauce, credits "the Spanish style" (W3: 1692) → street pizzerias adopt (W4: 1830s) → Margherita folklore (1889) → San Marzano DOP & "Made in Italy" brand — while durum wheat and paste now ride global trade back into Italy (W5).**

## Why Naples?
Vesuvian lava soil + poor-city tomato-first economics + Spanish-viceroyalty naming lag (the "Spanish style" credit) + 20th-c. emigrants who exported the brand (NY pizza = Italian-American = Italian again). The passport is Mexican; the flag was **manufactured in three steps: peasants, press, protected designation.**
