# Trace: Mexico → Manila (the Pacific backdoor)

## Route
**Acapulco (Mexican chili, maize, beans, squash, pineapple, turkey? — crop-only here) → galleon 1565–1815 → Manila/Naga (Bicol becomes the "Mexican province" of Asia) → from there: sweet potato to Fujian (1593, Chen Zhenlong's vines), maize & chili up the South China Sea, kabocha? later via Japanese/Mexico modern exchange — then the 19th-c. Chinese & Japanese diaspora carries *these Asian-naturalized* American crops onward (Peruvian chifa, Filipino *ginataang*-everything).**

## Why it matters
The **Manila door explains what the Goa door can't**: why China got the sweet potato *from the Pacific* and the chili gazetteers start on the southeast coast; why the Philippines' spiciest region is Catholic-Mexican-facing; why Bicol Express can honestly be called a trans-Pacific dish. 250 years of one convoy route = half of Asian "tradition."
