# Trace: Cassava — Brazil Feeds Congo (and Kerala)

## Route
**Brazil (Tupi: *manioc*, bitter-cyanide varieties → soaking/grating technology) → 1550s: Portuguese plant it on the Angola-Congo coast, *as ship food for the slave trade* → farmers carry seeds inland ahead of any state → 1600s–1800s: the forest frontier's "bank on the root" (drought-proof, pest-light, stores underground) → Belgian Congo forced-cultivation era → today DRC = world's top producer; Nigeria's gari/fufu economy; Kerala's *kappa/appa*; Thailand's industrial starch.**

## The dark engine
Cassava spread along the *provisioning geography of the slave trade* — the crop moved where ships needed cheap food. Naturalization came *despite* (and because of) that origin: African farmers, not the colonizers, made it a staple, and re-engineered it (fermentation, *gari* toasting, *fufu* pounding — Brazilian technique, African refinement — *verify attribution*).
