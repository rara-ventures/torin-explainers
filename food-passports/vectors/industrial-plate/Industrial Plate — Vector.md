# Vector: Industrial Plate — canning, hybrids, brands (W5)

> The last 125 years completed the naturalization by **erasing the route from memory**. Appert's canning 1800s → Heinz ketchup 1876 (a "condiment" that's 100% New World), refrigerated rail/steam, frozen pizza 1957 (Celentano — *verify*), instant ramen 1958/71, Green Revolution hybrids: CIMMYT wheat/maize & potato programs reaching India/Nepal 1960s–70s, palm-oil fried noodles tying SE Asia into the snack world. Industrial food *finishes* what the ships started: the crop becomes a **brand**, and brands have no memory — that's why the tomato is now, legally, Italian.
