# Vector: Silk Roads & Monsoon — the pre-1492 network (connections)

> This lab is Columbian-Exchange-centric, but the exchange only makes sense against the **older circuits**: wheat's 4,000-year westward crawl, sugarcane's New Guinea→India→Islam→Mediterranean run, Austronesian banana/coconut colonizing the Indian Ocean, and the pepper monsoon that is the *reason* 1492 happened at all. Use this vector to answer the inevitable comment: "pasta is older than Columbus" — true! And that's the point: foods always traveled; 1492 just doubled the planet's pantry and swapped its halves.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | caravan + monsoon sailing; crop diffusion by many hands, no single empire |
| **Period** | 3000 BCE → 1500 CE |
| **Key transfers** | wheat/barley west & east; sugarcane + sugar tech westward; banana/plantain into Africa; citrus & rice movements; pepper (Piper) = the spice everyone sought |
