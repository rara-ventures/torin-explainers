# Vector: Estado da Índia — The Goa Door (c. 1498–1700)

> The Portuguese maritime empire (Lisbon–Cape–Goa–Malacca–Macau–Nagasaki) was the exchange's **retail network**: small ships, many ports, gardens along the way. New World crops entered Asia through *it*, not through Spain — chili to Goa c. 1500, then to Kerala; cassava on the Angola & Brazil-India shuttle; papaya/cashew/pineapple planted on Goan roadsides in the 1560s. Wherever the feitoria went, the *achar* followed: vindaloo = Goa's *vin d'alho* (wine & garlic) pickling logic grafted onto chili — the word itself is the vector's fossil.
