# Vector: Plantations & Slavery — the forced conveyor

> The exchange's brutal engine: plantation (monocrop + enslaved labor) invented **food as industry** — sugar first (Mintz), then coffee, cacao, tobacco, cotton-by-proxy. And enslaved people were both cargo and *carriers*: provisioning crops (cassava, maize, rice — Carolina's rice was African expertise — *verify*) moved with them. The vector explains diffusion *speed* where documents don't: cassava's inland race through Congo, groundnut's rise in Senegambia, rice techniques in the US Lowcountry.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | plantation geography + slave-trade provisioning + colonial cash-crop policy |
| **Period** | 1500s (Madeira/Brazil sugar) → 1888 (Brazil abolition) → cash-crop colonialism to 1960s |
| **Dual movement** | crops *to* enslaved (provisioning) and *with* enslaved (knowledge: rice, processing tech) |
| **Mintz's point** | sugar went luxury → habit → calorie of the industrial working class: a class story in one crop |
