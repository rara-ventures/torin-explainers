# Vector: Migration & Diaspora Kitchens — W4–W5 second passport

> When people move, the pantry follows. Italian diaspora → NY pizza → global "Italian food"; Chinese diaspora → chifa (Peru), *arroz chaufa*; Japanese diaspora → Brazil's veg revolution & nikkei cuisine; Indian diaspora → curry to Britain → *chicken tikka masala* "national dish" joke (verify origin claims); Nepali & Bangladeshi labor → the global *dal-bhat* diaspora economy. Diaspora is how an already-naturalized crop **re-immigrates with marketing attached** — the tomato leaves Mexico, becomes Italian, then re-enters the world as "Italian." This is the vector behind every "founder story" episode.
