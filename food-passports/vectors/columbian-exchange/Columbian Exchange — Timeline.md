# Timeline — Vector: Columbian Exchange (ships)

|---|---|---|

| 1493 | 2nd voyage seeds (wheat, sugar, melons); Hispaniola plantings | E |
| 1519 | Cortés; maize/chili/tomato/cacao described to Europe | W |
| 1565/71 | Manila galleon regular: Mexican crops seed Pacific Asia | W→Asia |
| 1600s | potato/maize/cassava/peanut naturalizing across three continents | both |
| 1700s | states push potato; populations push maize into Africa & China | both |
