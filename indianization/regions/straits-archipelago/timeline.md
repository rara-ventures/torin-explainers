# Timeline — Straits Archipelago — Kedah → Singapore → Timor (500 BCE–1511)

| Year | Event | Indianization Link | Source |
|------|-------|--------------------|--------|
| 535 BCE–2nd c. CE | Sungai Batu, Bujang Valley — 17 bloomery workshops, >275k slag, 1150–1200°C | Iron magnet draws Tamil navigators, needs Brahmin admin | Naizatul & Mokhtar 2018 |
| c. 100–200 CE | Kadah (Kataha) — Tamil Sangam Pattinappalai & Ptolemy Takola (Kra Isthmus) | Tamil merchants bring Brahmi/Shaivite names | Nilakantha Sastri; Ptolemy |
| 2nd–3rd c. CE | Sungai Batu SB2A/B — Pallava script ye dharma, earliest brick monument C14 110 CE | Monks/Brahmins arrive with merchandise, Pallava script | CGAR 2009-2021 |
| c. 500–515 CE | Langkasuka — Liangshu embassy to Liang China, Bhogavarman, Brahmanical | Brahmins welcome Chinese envoys | Wheatley; Coedès 1968 |
| 671, 775 CE | Srivijaya overlordship — Yijing 671, Ligor 775 Sailendra banner over Kedah | Srivijaya-Mahayana uses Kedah as entrepôt | Coedès 1918 |
| c. 800–1200 CE | Bujang Valley candi florescence — 87 sites, 55 brick candi, Site 16 Shaiva, Sungai Mas Buddhist tablets | Tamil Shaiva acharyas + monks, local molding | Wales 1940; Lamb 1961 |
| 1025 & 1068 CE | Chola raids on Kadaram — Rajendra Thanjavur, captures Sangrama Vijayottunggavarman | Tamil naval breaks Srivijaya toll, guild Ayyavole spreads | Thanjavur; Hall 1985 |
| 1225 / c. 1350 CE | Timor in Zhufan Zhi (Zhao Rugua 1225 Ti-wu) & Daoyi Zhilue (Wang Dayuan c.1350 sandalwood, 12 ports) | Timor sandalwood demand China/India, Javanese/Gujarati carriers | Zhao Rugua; Ptak 1987 |
| c. 1300–1400 CE | Temasek (Singapore) — Fort Canning palace + shrine terraces, 85ha, 400k+ sherds, Indian carnelian | Javanese/Siamese/South Indian/Chinese co-reside, Indic via Java/Srivijaya | Miksic 1985-2013 |
| 1398–1402 CE | Fall of Singapura → Melaka founded by Parameswara (Paramesvara = Shiva) c.1400-02 | Retains Hindu title, Sanskrit court, shahbandar | Sejarah Melayu; Wade & Sun |
| 1414–1446 CE | Islamization of Melaka — Parameswara/Megat Iskandar converts c.1414 via Pasai | Qadi replaces Brahmin but Sanskrit undang-undang persists | Wake 1964; Winstedt |
| 1490s–1511 CE | Height & Portuguese capture — Melaka stock holds 1000 bahar sandalwood (Gujaratis), direct Macao-Timor after 1550s | Gujarati/Tamil/Chinese Hui institutionalize law, Sanskrit Malay | Barbosa; Ptak 1983 |
