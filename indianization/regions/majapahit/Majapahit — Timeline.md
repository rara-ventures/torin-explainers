# Timeline — Majapahit & Successors (Java–Malay)

| Year | Event | Indianization Link | Source | Confidence |
|------|-------|--------------------|--------|------------|
| 1222 CE | Ken Arok defeats Kediri at Genter and founds Singhasari at Tumapel, beginning the Rajasa line from which Majapahit descends | First Javanese court to make Sanskrit-derived royal titulature and the Śaiva–Buddhist cult a dynastic inheritance | Pararaton; Coedès 1968 | medium |
| 1275 CE | Kertanagara launches the Pamalayu expedition against the Srivijayan lands of Sumatra | Naval projection of a Sanskrit-titled Javanese mandala; Singhasari claims the maritime straits | Nagarakretagama; Coedès 1968 | medium |
| 1284 CE | Pabali expedition subdues Bali; its queen is captured and brought before the king (Nagarakretagama 42.1) | Extends the Javanese Siwa–Buddha court model to Bali a generation before Majapahit | Nagarakretagama 42.1; Robson 1995 | high |
| 1289 CE | Kertanagara refuses Kublai Khan's tribute demand and mutilates the envoy Meng Qi | Diplomatic theatre in the idiom of sacral kingship; precipitates the Yuan expedition | Yuan shi; Coedès 1968 | medium |
| 1292 CE | Jayakatwang of Kediri revolts, sacks Kutaraja and kills Kertanagara; Raden Wijaya withdraws to the Tarik forest | A succession crisis inside the same Indianized dynastic system, not an outside conquest | Pararaton; Coedès 1968 | medium |
| 1293 CE | Yuan fleet reaches Tuban; Wijaya allies with it, takes Daha, then expels the Mongols on 31 May; he is consecrated Kertarajasa Jayawardhana in November (10 or 12 Nov, contested) | The new capital at Trowulan reproduces the temple-mountain and the Sanskrit regnal name | Yuan shi; Coedès 1968 | high |
| 1294 CE | Kudadu inscription rewards the village that sheltered Wijaya during his flight | Old Javanese charter with Sanskrit formulas; sīma grant practice already fully formed | Kudadu inscription; Pigeaud 1960 | high |
| 1328 CE | Jayanagara is murdered by the court physician Tanca; Tribhuwana Wijayatunggadewi accedes with Gayatri Rajapatni as eminence | A queen regnant rules through an Indian-derived title and Śaiva–Buddhist legitimacy | Pararaton; Coedès 1968 | medium |
| 1336 CE | Tribhuwana appoints Gajah Mada mahāpatih; he takes the Palapa oath (dated 1334 or 1336) | Oath framed in Old Javanese with Sanskrit-derived titles; a cakravartin programme of universal rule | Pararaton; Robson 1995 | low |
| 1343 CE | Gajah Mada's forces defeat Bedulu and install a vassal line at Samprangan, Bali | Exports the Majapahit Siwa–Buddha court and varna-derived lordship to Bali | Babad Dalem; Nagarakretagama 14 | medium |
| 1365 CE | Prapanca completes the Nagarakretagama (Deśavarṇana), describing the capital and 98 tributaries | Sanskrit kāvya metre and cosmology used to map a Javanese imperial order | Nagarakretagama; Robson 1995 | high |
| 1389 CE | Hayam Wuruk dies; Wikramawardhana succeeds | End of the peak reign; succession disputed between rival kin-lords | Pararaton; Ricklefs 2008 | medium |
| 1404–1406 CE | Regreg (Paregreg) war between the western court and Bhre Wirabhumi's eastern court in Blambangan | Civil war fought with the same sacred regalia and titulature; drains the mandala's treasury | Pararaton; Ricklefs 2008 | medium |
| 1478 CE | Ranawijaya's army breaches Trowulan and kills Kertabhumi, briefly reuniting the court | Śaka chronogram date; Indian-derived calendrical legitimation rather than an annalistic record | Jiwu and Petak inscriptions; Pararaton | medium |
| 1527 CE | Demak takes Daha; courtiers, priests and artisans flee to Bali | The Islamic sultanate inherits the Sanskritic repertoire of rule; Bali becomes the ritual archive | Ricklefs 2008; Babad Dalem | medium |
