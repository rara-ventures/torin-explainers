# Timeline — Gandhara Corridor — Pakistan & Afghanistan

| Year | Event | Indianization Link | Source |
|------|-------|--------------------|--------|
| c. 518 BCE | Achaemenid conquest of Gandara — Darius I Behistun lists Gadāra as 7th satrapy, Taxila satrapal capital | Aramaic → Kharosthi script | Behistun DB I.16 |
| 326 BCE | Alexander at Taxila — Ambhi surrenders, founds Bucephala, leaves garrison | Hellenistic cities → Indo-Greek synthesis | Arrian Anabasis 5.8; Marshall Taxila |
| c. 268–232 BCE | Ashoka Rock Edicts at Shahbazgarhi & Mansehra in Kharosthi/Gāndhārī Prakrit | First epigraphic Indianization, Dhamma | Hultzsch; Falk |
| c. 180–130 BCE | Indo-Greek Menander I (Milinda) at Taxila — Milindapanha conversion, bilingual coins | Greek king adopts Buddhism, Kharosthi+Greek | Bopearachchi; Milinda Panha |
| c. 50 CE | Periplus §38-44: Barbaricum at Indus, Bimaran Casket (Jalalabad) — first human Buddha | Roman-Indian trade, aniconic→human Buddha | Casson Periplus; British Museum |
| 78/127 CE | Kanishka I founds Kushan Empire, Puruṣapura (Peshawar), Rabatak Inscription, 4th Council | Kushan Sanskritization, 400ft stupa | Sims-Williams Rabatak; Falk |
| c. 100–300 CE | Mature Gandhara art: Takht-i-Bahi (46 CE), Corinthian Buddha, stucco | Visual language exported to Bamiyan/Dunhuang | Marshall; Zwalf BM |
| c. 233–365 CE | Sasanian Kushanshahr — Shapur I SKZ claims Hindustan to Peshawar, Shiva/Nandi coins | Sasanian pearl roundels → Bamiyan/Tang | Grenet; Rezakhani |
| c. 100–300 CE | Gāndhārī Kharosthi scrolls (British Library) → Lokakṣema (147–189) translates in Luoyang | Gandhara → China via Kharosthi | Salomon; Zacchetti |
| 399–414 CE | Faxian pilgrimage — describes Taxila ruined but Peshawar stupa 40+ zhang, 1000 monks | Chinese eyewitness at zenith | Legge Foguo Ji |
| 507 & 554 CE | Bamiyan Buddhas carved (38m E, 55m W) — C14, Sasanian–Gupta murals | Westernmost Indianization, links to Tarim | Blänsdorf 2009; Xuanzang |
| c. 455–565 & 629–645 | Hephthalite destruction (Mihirakula) + Xuanzang finds 1000 monasteries empty | Rupture, shift to Himalayas/Indonesia | Marshall; Beal Si-Yu-Ki |
