# Vector: Buddhism — Theravada / Mahayana / Vajrayana

From Amaravati → Dvaravati Theravada, Nalanda → Srivijaya Mahayana, Pala → Sailendra Vajrayana, Sri Lanka → Pagan → Sukhothai Theravada turn. Your Indonesia focus.
