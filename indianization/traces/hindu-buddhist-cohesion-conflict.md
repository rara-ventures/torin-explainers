# Trace — Hindu–Buddhist Cohesion & Conflict

> **Thesis:** Hindu (Shaiva/Vaishnava + Brahmin devaraja) and Buddhist (Theravada/Mahayana/Vajrayana) were not sequential religions but **co-existing grammars** that Indianization carried together. Vault thesis `thesis.md` says ≥2 of script/law/kingship/cult = Indianized — both cults used same Sanskrit kingship. Cohesion was dyarchy/syncretism; conflict was purge/competition for royal patronage. **Verified anchors 2026-10-31:** Kalasan 778 ✓, Angkor K.235 802 ✓, Kalyani 1472 ✓, Mahavamsa 36.111 ✓, Swayambhu 1751 ✓.

## Pattern

> **Cohesion where king needed both**: Sailendra needed Gaudi Brahmins for `varman` title even while building Borobudur; Ayutthaya needed Brahmins for coronation even after Theravada state; Nepal needed both for varna + merit. **Conflict where patronage shifted**: Pagan needed Thaton Canon to legitimize Theravada over Ari; Chola needed to break Srivijaya toll; Lanka needed single nikaya for state control. Same Sanskrit kingship grammar, competing cults.
