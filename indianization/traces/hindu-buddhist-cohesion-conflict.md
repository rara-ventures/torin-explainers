# Trace — Hindu–Buddhist Cohesion & Conflict

> **Thesis:** Hindu (Shaiva/Vaishnava + Brahmin devaraja) and Buddhist (Theravada/Mahayana/Vajrayana) were not sequential religions but **co-existing grammars** that Indianization carried together. Vault thesis `thesis.md` says ≥2 of script/law/kingship/cult = Indianized — both cults used same Sanskrit kingship. Cohesion was dyarchy/syncretism; conflict was purge/competition for royal patronage.

## Cohesion — Dyarchy & Syncretism

| Year | Place | Cohesion Form | Evidence | Source |
|------|-------|---------------|----------|--------|
| 875 CE | Champa Dong Duong (Indrapura) | **Paramabuddhaloka** — Indravarman II fuses Laksmindralokesvara (Avalokiteshvara) + Shaiva linga, Brahmins serve Mahayana king | C.66; Griffiths | high |
| 778–856 CE | Java Sailendra–Sanjaya | **Dyarchy**: Sailendra Borobudur (Vajradhatu mandala, Gaudi monks) + Sanjaya Prambanan (Shivagrha 856, Rakai Pikatan) coexist 10km apart, share artisans, Balaputra funds Nalanda after exile | Kalasan 778; Kelurak 782; Prambanan 856; Epigraphia Indica XVII | high |
| c. 1350 CE | Ayutthaya | **Thammasat dyarchy** — Theravada sangha + Khmer Brahmin *purohita* co-crown king, Dharmaśāstra + Pali vinaya both law | Baker & Phongpaichit | medium |
| 13th–14th c. | Sukhothai/Angkor | **Lankavamsa + devaraja** — Ramkhamhaeng uses Pali Tipitaka but Brahmins perform devaraja coronation; Angkor Wat converts 1350-1431 Buddha replaces Vishnu yet Brahmins remain | Griswold; Woodward | medium |
| c. 800–1100 CE | Kedah Bujang Valley | **Parallel candi** — Sungai Mas Buddhist tablets + Site 16 Shaiva linga co-exist, Tamil monks + Shaiva acharyas share entrepôt | Lamb 1961; Wales | medium |

## Conflict — Purge & Competition

| Year | Place | Conflict Form | Evidence | Source |
|------|-------|---------------|----------|--------|
| 1056–1057 CE | Pagan (Myanmar) | **Ari purge** — Anawrahta via Shin Arahan (Mon) converts Ari (tantric) Buddhism → Theravada, sacks Thaton for Pali Canon (32 white elephants) | Glass Palace Chronicle; Kalyani Inscriptions | medium |
| 1181–1218 CE | Angkor | **Mahayana vs Shaiva** — Jayavarman VII Bayon (Hevajra/Buddha) replaces earlier Shaiva devaraja (Jayavarman II 802), then Theravada replaces Mahayana 14th c. | Freeman & Jacques; Coedès | high |
| 1025 CE | Straits | **Vaishnava Chola vs Mahayana Srivijaya** — Rajendra I raids Kadaram (Kedah), captures Sailendra king, Shaiva Chola breaks Buddhist Srivijaya toll | Thanjavur inscription | high |
| 982 CE | Champa | **Sinic vs Indic** — Lê Hoàn destroys Indrapura (Mahayana) 982, Shaiva south persists, Dai Viet Thiền later state (Lý 1010) replaces Cham Buddhism with Sinic Mahayana | Maspero; Hall | medium |
| 1153–1186 CE | Sri Lanka | **Mahavihara purification** — Parakramabahu I purifies sangha, suppresses Abhayagiri Mahayana, standardizes Theravada Lankavamsa exported to Pagan/Sukhothai | Culavamsa | high |

## Pattern

> **Cohesion where king needed both**: Sailendra needed Gaudi Brahmins for `varman` title even while building Borobudur; Ayutthaya needed Brahmins for coronation even after Theravada state. **Conflict where patronage shifted**: Pagan needed Thaton Canon to legitimize new Theravada over Ari; Chola needed to break Srivijaya Buddhist toll for Tamil Shaiva trade. Same Sanskrit kingship grammar, competing cults.
