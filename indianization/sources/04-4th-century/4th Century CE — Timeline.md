# Timeline — 04 4Th Century

| Year | Event | Region | Source | Confidence |
|------|-------|--------|--------|------------|
| 319–320 CE | The Gupta era begins under Chandragupta I; the Gupta–Licchavi marriage alliance creates the north Indian court model later copied in Southeast Asia | North India | Gupta inscriptions; Majumdar 1981 | high |
| c. 335 CE | Samudragupta accedes (dates proposed range 319–350); he turns the Gupta court into the most self-conscious Sanskrit monarchy of the age | North India | Allahabad pillar; Sharma 1989 | medium |
| 347 CE | Fan Wen attacks Jin-held Jiaozhou with 40,000–50,000 troops, opening a century of Cham–Chinese war | Champa / Jiaozhou | Liang shu; Kiernan 2019 | medium |
| c. 350–370 CE | Harishena's Allahabad pillar *prashasti* records Samudragupta's southern march, the defeat and reinstatement of twelve Dakṣiṇāpatha kings including Vishnugopa of Kanchi | North India / Kanchi | Allahabad pillar; Fleet 1888; Pollock 2006 | high |
| c. 350–400 CE | The Đông Yên Châu (C.174) inscription, the oldest substantial Old Cham text, invokes a Nāga in Pallava script and ends with a thousand-year hell curse | Trà Kiệu (Champa) | C.174; Schweyer 2011; Griffiths 2014 | medium |
| c. 352–379 CE | The Buddha's tooth relic is installed at Anuradhapura under Sirimeghavanna, making it a palladium of Sinhala kingship (his reign is dated 301–328 or 352–379) | Sri Lanka | Mahavamsa; Majumdar 1981 | low |
| 357 CE | Funan's king Chandana (Tiānzhú Zhāntán) sends tamed elephants to the Jin emperor Mu | Funan → Jin China | Liang shu; Pelliot 1903 | medium |

| c. 380–400 CE | Bhadravarman founds the Mỹ Sơn sanctuary, dedicating the valley to the linga Bhadreśvara; C.72 fixes four boundaries, a tithe and a curse on trespass | Champa (Mỹ Sơn) | C.72; Finot 1902; Majumdar 1927 | high |
| 399 CE | Bhadravarman campaigns in Rinan and Jiuzhen and briefly seizes the coast | Champa / Jiaozhou | Liang shu; Maspero 2002 | medium |
| 399 CE | Faxian leaves Chang'an for India in search of Vinaya texts, beginning a fourteen-year pilgrimage | China → India | Faxian, *Foguoji*; Legge 1886 | high |
| c. 400 CE | Kanchipuram's Pallava chancery becomes the model for Cham and Khmer epigraphy; Pallava Grantha is exported with the Sanskrit package | Kanchi → Southeast Asia | Pallava inscriptions; de Casparis 1956 | medium |
| 405–413 CE | Bhadravarman's long war with the Chinese governor Du Xue ends in his defeat and disappearance; his sons are killed and Champa falls into civil war | Champa / Jiaozhou | Liang shu; Chapuis 1995; Vickery 1998 | medium |
