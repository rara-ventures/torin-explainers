# Timeline — 14th Century CE

| 1353 | Ayutthaya takes Angkor after the 1352 siege and installs a son on the Khmer throne, holding it until 1357 | Angkor | Ayutthaya chronicles; Coedès 1968 | medium |
| 1357 | Bubat massacre: the Sundanese royal party is killed at Trowulan after the marriage negotiation collapses; Gajah Mada is disgraced | Trowulan | *Pararaton*; *Kidung Sunda* | medium |
| 1361 (or 1331) | Sumana Thera installs the Sinhala Mahāvihāra ordination at Wat Pa Mamuang, Sukhothai, transmitted through Mon Martaban | Sukhothai | *Jinakālamālī*; Jayawickrama 1968 | low |
| 1365 | Prapanca completes the *Deśavarṇana* (*Nagarakretagama*), listing ninety-eight tributaries and the Trowulan ceremonial calendar | Trowulan | Prapanca; Robson 1995 | high |
| 1371–1377 | Chế Bồng Nga sacks Thăng Long in 1371 and twice in 1377; the Trần ruler Duệ Tông is killed in a failed assault on Vijaya | Champa / Đại Việt | *Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư*; Coedès 1968 | high |
| 1389–1390 | Hayam Wuruk dies in 1389, opening the succession split; Chế Bồng Nga is killed in 1390, ending Cham pressure on Đại Việt | Trowulan / Champa | *Pararaton*; *Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư* | high |
| 1398 | Majapahit sacks Singapura; Parameswara flees via Muar to the Bertam river, founding Melaka c. 1400 | Temasek → Melaka | *Sejarah Melayu*; Miksic 2013 | medium |
