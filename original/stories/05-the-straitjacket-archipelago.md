## The Idea: Sovereignty Is Plumbing

Control the gap, you control the flow. Control the enclave, you own the exception. Every story here is a valve or a bubble.

## The Chokepoints

**Ryukyu Islands — The 1,200 km Wall** (`how-did-japan-end-up-controlling-chinas-gateway-to-the-pacific.md`)
Japan's southwestern chain from Kyushu to Taiwan is **1,200 km** of islands sitting across the People's Liberation Army Navy's only free route to the Pacific. In peace, Chinese warships legally thread the Miyako Strait (where Japan scrambled jets daily in 2025). In war, missiles on Yonaguni (110 km from Taiwan — US Marines emplaced launchers Oct 2026), Miyako, Amami, Ishigaki, and long-range anti-ship missiles at Camp Kengun (Mar 2026, reaching mainland China) could seal it.

History hack: Dual vassalage (1609 Satsuma invasion hidden from Beijing because tribute missions were Japan's backdoor into Ming/Qing trade → 1879 annexation as Okinawa Prefecture despite Qing protests → 1945 Battle of Okinawa: 50k US casualties, 94k Japanese + 94k civilians dead → 1951–72 US military government → 25k US troops still, 70% of US-exclusive land in Japan).

China's wedge today: fund Okinawan indigeneity discourse at UN (only 3% want independence, 2022 poll).

**Yellow Sea — The Shallow Sea That Decides Korea** (`why-is-the-yellow-sea-so-important.md`)
Avg depth 44 m (Baltic-like), but holds Incheon (Seoul's harbor), Chinese Bohai oil, and North Korea's Nampo. China's nine-dash-style claim vs Korea's EEZ. The 1953 Northern Limit Line (UN commander drew it, North Korea never accepted) → Yeonpyeong shellings. Torin links it to Ryukyu: First Island Chain's northern valve. If China holds the Yellow Sea, it outflanks Seoul without crossing the DMZ.

**Great Lakes — The Freshwater Sea Two Countries Share** (`how-long-can-the-us-and-canada-keep-sharing-the-great-lakes.md`)
20% of world's surface fresh water, 84M people, treaty since 1909 Boundary Waters Treaty. Next stress: Line 5 pipeline (Enbridge) under the Straits of Mackinac — 1953 pipe carrying 540k bpd Canadian oil, shut-down battle 2021-26. Lesson: chokepoint inside friendship.

**Sri Lanka — The Teardrop on the Shipping Lane** (`why-is-sri-lanka-so-important.md`)
8% of global trade passes its south coast. Hambantota port (2017 99-year lease to China Merchants after debt) sits across from Tamil Nadu (India's Dravidian politics). 2022 collapse made the valve visible: whoever funds the port owns the lane.

**Micronesia/Guam/Timor-Leste — The Second Chain's Deep Water** (see Immortals)
FSM's 3M km² EEZ vs Timor's Wetar/Ombai deep-water sub transit vs Guam's harbor. Three answers to one US-China question: where can a nuclear sub hide?

**Denmark's Straits** (from Immortals story: $60B/yr Russian oil via 1.6M bpd shadow fleet, 1857 tolls banned → inspection hack).

> **Crazy synthesis:** Ryukyu + Yellow Sea + Denmark do the same math: *legal in peace, lethal in war*. All rely on "innocent passage" that missiles can revoke.

## The Exclaves & Enclaves

**Kaliningrad — The Russian Island Inside NATO** (`how-does-russias-kaliningrad-survive-surrounded-by-nato.md`)
15k km² (Lithuania + Poland surround), ~1M people, ice-free Baltic port (Baltiysk). History: Teutonic Knights → Prussia's heart → 1945 Potsdam gave Königsberg to USSR as war prize → depopulated, repopulated. Survives via Suwalki Gap (65 km Lithuania-Poland neck) + Baltic Sea lane. NATO's nightmare valve.

**India-Bangladesh — The World's Most Absurd Border, Fixed in 2015** (`why-is-the-border-between-india-and-bangladesh-such-a-mess.md`)
Before 2015: **106 Indian enclaves in Bangladesh + 92 Bangladeshi enclaves in India**, including 21 counter-enclaves (enclaves inside enclaves) and the legendary **Dahala Khagrabari** — the world's only *counter-counter-enclave* (Indian field inside Bangladeshi enclave inside Indian enclave inside Bangladesh). Pop. ~50,000 stateless. Root: Mughal land grants to Cooch Behar vs Mughal emperor, frozen by 1947 Radcliffe Line without survey. Fix: 2015 Land Boundary Agreement swapped enclaves, gave citizenship.
Verification: Most complex border ever surveyed; mapping by Brendan Whyte.

**The Gambia + Eswatini + Lesotho** (Enclave triptych)
- Gambia: 10 km buffer around a river (1889).
- Eswatini: Why *isn't* it part of South Africa? Because British needed a Swazi buffer against Boers, Queen Labotsibeni maneuvered 1890s concessions, 1968 independence with Sobhuza II's 82-year reign; survives via SACU customs + South African rand peg (like Bhutan).
- Lesotho implied (not in set, but Rwanda/Burundi hill logic applies — enclaves survive when annexation costs > taxes).

**Ceuta & Melilla — Spain Is an African Country** (`why-is-spain-an-african-country.md`)
Two autonomous cities on Morocco's Mediterranean coast: Ceuta (since 1580), Melilla (1497). EU + NATO outside Europe, fences topped with anti-climb netting, migration valve.

Connection: Both are *reverse Kaliningrads* — European exclaves that make Spain an African power *and* an African border guard for Brussels.

## The Islands Everyone Wants to Borrow

**Falklands — Sheep That Control Oceans** (`why-are-the-falkland-islands-disproportionately-important-to-geopolitics.md`)
3,000 people, 1,700 sheep per human? Actually ~500k sheep. Claim: proximity to Antarctica, South Atlantic–Pacific chokepoint, potential oil. 1982 war proof: Thatcher sent a task force 8,000 miles for islands most Britons couldn't find. Principle: *sovereignty by sheep-ship* — if you abandon an island, someone else's EEZ eats 400k km² of sea.

**San Marino's Monte Titano vs Falklands' Mount Pleasant:** Both survive by *being* the airstrip.

## Synthesis: Three Laws of the Jacket

1. **Depth matters:** Deep water (Timor, Micronesia) hides subs; shallow water (Yellow Sea) hides claims; narrow water (Danish Straits, Ryukyu gaps) hides leverage.
2. **Enclaves remember:** The most intricate border (India-Bangladesh) took a 2015 treaty to fix 1947's pencil line; the simplest (straight lines in Sahara) still bleed because they ignored mountains (Rwanda) or rivers (Gambia).
3. **Valves outlive owners:** Ryukyu swapped China→Satsuma→Japan→US→Japan; Kaliningrad Königsberg→Soviet; Hambantota British→Sri Lankan→Chinese lease — the gap remains, the landlord changes.

> Craziest link: **Guam (200 mi²)** and **Kaliningrad (5,800 mi²)** are strategic twins — both militarized islands whose residents didn't vote to host the missiles. Guam is 41% defense GDP; Kaliningrad is 100% Iskander GDP. Both exist because a 1945 Potsdam line and a 1898 gunboat salute decided 2026 targeting.
