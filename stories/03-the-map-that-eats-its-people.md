# The Map That Eats Its People: Africa's Engineered Chaos

> 12 explainers, one thesis: borders don't fail *despite* design. They fail *by* design.

**Sources:** Nigeria, Cameroon, DRC, Rwanda/Burundi, Eritrea, South Sudan, Ghana, Botswana, The Gambia, Madagascar, Malawi — plus Luxembourg as mirror. Files in `../sources/`.

## Act I — The Lines (Two Rivers That Became Countries)

Open with the absurdities you can see from space:

**The Gambia — A River That Became a State**
11,000 km², 2.4 million people — smallest mainland African country — is *navigable water* that Britain bureaucratized. 1889 joint commission drew 10 km buffers north/south of the Gambia River + 10 km radius at Yarbutenda (limit of navigability), because Britain's Bathurst (1816) needed the river and France's Senegal needed the shore. Twice in the 1870s Britain tried to trade the whole Gambia to France for upper Niger; Parliament blocked it.

> **Verification:** 1,100 km river, half navigable; colony 1821; protectorate 1894 (35 chiefdoms); peanut monoculture; slavery abolished 1906. Senegambia Confederation (1982-89) — Senegal's 2,700 troops after 1981 coup while Charles & Diana married — collapsed because Gambia refused deeper union linking Senegal's Casamance.

Result: an enclave splitting Senegal in two, porous enough to host exiles (Cameroon's Tchiroma fled via Nigeria to The Gambia in 2025) and to flip Taiwan→China (cut 2013, Beijing waited for 2016 Tsai Ing-wen victory to resume — two months post-election, pre-inauguration — as leverage).

**Cameroon — Four Colonial Layers Stacked**
German Duala treaties July 1884 → WWI split 4/5 France, 1/5 Britain with no reference to Sokoto/Adamawa Emirates or Bamileke grasslands → French counterinsurgency vs UPC (1948, banned 1955, tens of thousands killed with Indochina/Algeria tactics) → 1960 independence under Ahidjo → 1961 plebiscite (Northern Cameroons joined Nigeria) → 1972 referendum **99.99%** to abolish federation (pre-drafted centralist constitution). Biya (b. 1933, world's oldest head) has run since 1982: 1984 coup attempt, 2016 Anglophone protests → 2017 Ambazonia, 46 leaders rendered from Abuja in 2018 (Nigerian court ruled it illegal). Age median 18, 42% <15, 1M displaced + 425k refugees. China holds 65% bilateral debt, built Kribi deepwater port.

**Rwanda/Burundi — The Counterfactual That Proves the Rule**
Only African borders that are *not* colonial aggregates: volcanic Albertine Rift hills so dense and centralized (Rwanda's Tutsi monarchy with youth militias, Burundi's 4 Ganwa lineages outside Hutu/Tutsi) that expanding was costlier than taxing. Germans kept them as suzerainties (1894), Belgians made them one territory but racialized fluid status into race via mandatory ID cards (1930s). 1961 assassination of Burundi's Rwagasore two weeks after landslide, 1962 UN Addis federation attempt failed — diverging into Hutu republic (Rwanda) vs Ganwa monarchy (Burundi).

Lesson: even *right-sized* borders explode if you harden identity.

## Act II — The Prize (When Wealth Finances Fragmentation)

Each prize sustains a different trap.

**DRC — 73% of Cobalt That Funds 100 Militias**
900,000 sq mi (Mississippi east of it), 112M, 200+ languages. 73% world cobalt (70-80% China-refined), 40% coltan, Rubaya mine = 15% world coltan, gold smuggled via Rwanda. Leopold II's 1885 private property → Belgian colony → Mobutu's Zaire → Rwanda's 1996/98 invasions chasing Hutu génocidaires → Second Congo War (1998-2003) killed **5.4 million** (mostly disease/starvation) under 100+ armed groups — Africa's deadliest since WWII. M23 (Rwanda-backed) 2012 → crushed 2013 by UN Force Intervention Brigade → reborn 2021, occupying Kivu.

2025 pattern: Feb Tshisekedi *minerals-for-security* letter to Trump; June Washington deal (DRC+Rwanda) + US Strategic Assets Reserve; July Qatar M23-DRC ceasefire; **1,500 civilians killed since Dec 2025 anyway**. Minerals keep flowing to China/US while people don't.

**Botswana vs Malawi vs Madagascar — Controlled Experiment**

| Country | Start condition | Prize | Result |
|---------|----------------|-------|--------|
| **Botswana** | Capable Tswana kgotla institutions *before* diamonds (1885 protectorate to block Rhodes, 1895 chiefs' London trip, 1967 Mines Act nationalized tribal minerals) | Orapa diamonds 1967; Debswana 50/50; diamonds 1/3 revenue, 80% exports | Saved longest (Pula Fund), but broke anyway: 2023 lab-grown + China demand crash; 2024 UDC ended 58-year BDP rule (4 seats left) |
| **Malawi** | Mission elite (1912 Nyasaland Native Association) + 1953 Rhodesia-Nyasaland Federation with European dominance | Tobacco >$500M now, 76% <$3/day, 80% maize | Never diversified; 40% budget donors, US aid -60% after USAID dismantled 2025; IMF $175M program terminated after 18 months, debt 88% GDP |
| **Madagascar** | Austronesian + Bantu, Merina highlanders vs cotiers divide exploited by France 1895 | Vanilla **80% world** ($600→$20/kg swing), mining 40% exports but <5% GDP | Cycle of CAPSAT kingmaker coups (2009, 2025 Gen Z uprising — CAPSAT refused to fire, Rajoelina fled on French plane, Oct 12 2025, AU suspended) |

> **Verification:** Madagascar vanilla 70-80% globe (FAO/Comtrade), price collapsed 2023-24 from cyclone overproduction. Botswana Debswana 50/50 verified via De Beers. DRC cobalt 73% via USGS.

**Eritrea — Geography Is the Mineral**
No constitution/legislature/election in 33 years; RSF 2024 press freedom *below* North Korea; indefinite conscription called enslavement. Yet courted by Russia (Massawa naval base talks since 2018), UAE (Assab base until 2021, then pivoted to Ethiopia), US (normalizing vs Houthis since Oct 2023), Egypt (Asmara pact vs Ethiopia GERD late 2024). Asset: **Assab + Massawa** — deepwater ports inside Bab el-Mandeb, **10% global trade** chokepoint.

Ports are its Pula Fund — but rents sustain repression, not development.

## Act III — The Bill (Who Pays)

**Nigeria — Patchwork Sovereignty**
Constitution promises federal monopoly on police/defense. Reality: 370k federal police (~25% guard VIPs, notoriously corrupt) replaced region-by-region:
- NE: 5-10k ISWAP (+ 26k Civilian Joint Task Force, born 2013 as vigilantes)
- NW: ~30k bandits, 8 kingpins killed 2024, Lakurawa militia (founded 2017 anti-bandit, now jihadist — target of **25 Dec 2025 US Tomahawks in Sokoto**, first ever US missile strike on Nigeria)
- Imo jailbreak 1,800 (2021), Amotekun (SW, 2020, 120 vehicles), Ebube Agu (SE, collapsed)

12 northern states run Sharia + Hisbah; Sahel states (Mali/Burkina/Niger) left ECOWAS Jan 2025 for Alliance of Sahel States; MNJTF (Cameroon/Chad/Benin) patrols a Lake Chad that shrank **-90% 1960s→1980s**. Parliament now debates 36 state police forces — solving fragmentation with fragmentation.

**South Sudan — Death by Pipeline**
World's newest (2011), grown from British condominium Sudan's north-south line drawn for Nile control. 90% budget is oil → single pipeline north through *Sudan's* civil war (RSF vs SAF since 2023). Feb 2024 heating stations failed → asphalt plug → **revenue zero**. Debt $2.2B (>50% GDP, oil-pre-sold years forward), soldiers unpaid. March 2025 White Army (Nuer militia) killed 250-400 soldiers including a UN heli crew; Machar rearrested (again) despite White Army denying his command. Uganda has troops 435 km inside violating UN embargo; China is dominant oil investor *and* UNMISS troop contributor.

**Ghana — The Democracy Tax**
Most stable in the coup belt (unbroken 1992 multiparty, 3 alternations since 2000) *because* Akan is 46% — big enough to anchor, too small to rule alone, forcing swing coalitions in middle belt/Accra/north. But stability is fiscally punished: every election triggers overspending → 2022 default (93% GDP debt, 54% inflation, *dumsor* blackouts). House of Chiefs parallel authority (Dagbon chief killed 2002, Bawku 300+ dead since 2021) absorbs shocks Ghana's state can't. Still 7th-largest UN peacekeeper supplier — *coup-proofing by exporting soldiers*. Unique 2025 move: sent envoy to Sahel juntas while Nigeria leaned to Washington — two bills for one Sahel collapse.

## Act IV — The Mirror (Luxembourg)

Same size as The Gambia (~2,600 vs 11,000 km²), opposite fate.

Luxembourg shrank 10,700 → 2,600 km² via three great-power amputations (1659 Pyrenees, 1815 Vienna, 1839 Belgium). Lost steel dominance (world highest per-capita 19th c., now 2%) → deliberately *engineered indispensability*: 1950 Coal & Steel Community founder (binding France/Germany via its furnaces) → 1963 first Eurobond → world's #2 fund hub → 1985 SES satellite → 2017 first EU space-resource law. Schengen was signed on its soil. GDP/capita €147k (inflated by foreign workers, so measures via GNI), NATO's smallest army (1,100) but hosts EU Court/EIB.

> **Verification:** Luxembourg GDP 2024 €146-147k (IMF), GNI correction standard. Senegal-Gambia divergence vs Luxembourg-Belgium convergence shows the fork: lines can become bridges *if* institutions are designed to monetize integration not extraction.

**Ending question:** Botswana had a Pula Fund; could it have become Luxembourg's fund hub? Ghana has a transit corridor for 3 landlocked Sahel states (Burkina/Mali/Niger) — could it host EU-style institutions like Luxembourg hosts for Europe? The map doesn't have to eat its people — but only if you build a state *before* you find the diamonds.
